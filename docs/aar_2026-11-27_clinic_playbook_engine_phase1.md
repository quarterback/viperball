## Assumptions and remaining gaps
The supplied instruction describes grounded kick passes at landing spot and subsequent kicks; RULES.md and current engine use the existing incomplete outcome and allow only one kick pass per play. Do not change these here. Snap kick is a drop kick for five, punt is distinct territorial action (pindown possible); no wedge or forward hand pass. Existing early-down kick policy is retained. Field zone is coarse, kick_range is a preference bucket not legal range. The new hazard is aggregate conditional on no interception; it is not per-transfer fumble/spacing simulation. Existing lateral accounting reports planned count on early termination, so do not treat target as completed transfers. No explicit tracking of player coordinates, trailing depth, true weak-side lane, alternate snap recipient, third-player run path, or live coverage exchange; Corridor Switch and Third Runner are executable existing territory/kick-lateral subtype preferences, not new spatial actions. The actual read branch is a coarse alignment-conditioned weighted choice, not an ordered post-snap decision tree. Existing defensive scouting, not new swarming/press mechanics, is reused. These are material gaps from the complete clinic architecture. Need shared participant/channel/transfer ledger, conditional sequencing, support and pressure assignment resolver, additional concepts and matched full-game calibration before claiming full clinic implementation.
