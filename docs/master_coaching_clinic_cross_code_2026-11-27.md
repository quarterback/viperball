# Master coaching clinic: cross-code playbook development
November 27, 2026 — implementation brief, not another research-only task

Commission: act as a Viperball coaching faculty drawing on Canadian and American football, rugby league and union, Gaelic and Australian football, association football, lacrosse, field hockey and handball. Develop executable attacks and counters. The source notes are embedded so agents without internet access can implement. The faculty's designs below are original proposals, not claims that the source sports play these exact calls. This document does not claim simulation validation.

## Chalkboard rules and current engine
Read RULES.md, COACHING_SPEC.md, docs/OFFENSE_SCHEMAS.md, docs/football_coaching_casebook_2026-11-27.md, docs/aar_2026-11-27_playbook_cookbook_review.md, engine/playbook_catalog.py and current data/playbooks JSON. Reconcile concurrent work before editing. The rulebook has 11 players, five minimum on line, Zeroback normally receives snap but any legally positioned player can do so, one player in motion at snap parallel or away, Viper set one second unless legal motion player, no forward hand pass, unlimited legal sideways/backward laterals, one kick pass per play, caught kick pass stays live, incomplete grounded kick pass dead, live dropped lateral, all positions free after snap. Six downs for 20 yards, kicks on any down, distinct miss rules. Five OL, one Z, one Viper, four flankers. Viper and flankers play both ways; three midfield lines, anchors, live substitutions through midfield with exiting player fully off first. Use actual personnel and fatigue. Preserve all six scores, Delta, bonus possession, penalties and injury rules. No CFL forward waggle at snap, multiple simultaneous motions at snap, rugby rucks, soccer forward hand passes, lacrosse screens that violate illegal screen/obstruction, AFL mark stoppage or automatic second kick pass. Do not confuse legal role interchange with permission to break formation or substitution rules. Reconcile contradictory rulebook passages (e.g. general kick-pass wording versus one-per-play, and lateral penalty descriptions) against current engine before implementing; no silent rules change.

Catalog currently supports formation tags, family tags, kick-pass subtype, roster/situation eligibility, compatible schools and counters. Package biases are not ordered instructions or a spatial resolver. The current catalog excludes scoring kicks and punts from package families: finish decisions remain in existing legal decision policy. A named package with unchanged generic weights is NOT implementation of a novel mechanism. Start with existing selector where enough, extend shared primitives where necessary, not a separate simulation.

## Faculty source notes (public research)
- CFL rulebook landing page https://cfl.prod.s.cfl.ca/cfl-rulebook ; CFLdb terminology https://www.cfldb.ca/glossary/on-field/ : running start from backfield timed to snap creates speed and a changing defensive picture. Translate acceleration AFTER snap or legal parallel/away motion, not CFL legality. CFL twist/stunt interchanges rush responsibilities; retain real assignment count.
- A-11 contemporary account https://www.gainesvilletimes.com/sports/holloway-is-new-offensive-scheme-genius-or-gimmick/ : Bryan/Humphries used sparse interior alignment, two potential QBs and late alignment to obscure eligible receivers under former rules. Viperball already allows all players to participate after snap. Keep five on line; exploit distributor identity and role uncertainty rather than import numbering loophole. 
- World Rugby https://www.world.rugby/news/483679/attacking-structure-how-pods-gain-the-hard-yards : 1-3-3-1 and 2-4-2 describe distribution of forwards across pitch, retaining strong central carries and wide support. Adapt pod function, not eight-forward formation or ruck recycling.
- Rugby league NRL obstruction interpretations https://www.nrl.com/siteassets/operations/documentation/nrl-laws-interpretations-2023.pdf : distinguishes lead/flat runners and sweep runners; decoys must not obstruct opponents. This is rules evidence, not a complete NRL playbook. Our six-down sequence is original; unlike league tackle counts, Viperball can renew downs by gaining 20 yards.
- Gaelic coaching federation https://connachtgaa.ie/principles-of-coaching/ : width stretches defense, depth provides short and long outlets, penetration needs timing, off-ball movement can open space for somebody else. Use support runners behind the ball, legal kick-pass targets ahead, and conditional finishing.
- AFL club coaching handbook https://play.afl/sites/default/files/2025-11/2.%20Club%20Coaching%20Handbook.pdf pp 58,60: time leads into space, dummy leads and scanning, switch play, crumb around contests, rotate players through set-play responsibilities. Viperball does not award a mark; no invented free kick or live grounded kick pass.
- FIFA third player https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/game/game-analysis/in-possession/third-player-combinations.php : initial pass, layoff, off-ball runner, decisive continuation; awareness and timing matter. Adapt forward delivery only as kick pass and layoff only backward/level.
- FIFA Ben Bartlett https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/elite-sessions/in-possession/switching--play.php : stretch before switching, use central anchor or diagonal delivery, choose retain vs switch, train in varied spaces.
- FIFA counterpress https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/elite-sessions/transition-to-defending/preparing-and-executing-counter-pressing.php and https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/talent-coach-programme/press-and-regain/3v3-plus-3-possession-game-pressing-and-switching-play.php : first pressure, cover lanes, stagger depth and secure behind. Apply only while Viperball turnover return remains live or on legal next snap; no soccer-style ongoing possession after dead ball.
- Lacrosse coach resource https://laxlibrary.com/defending-two-man-game-pick-roll-defense-communication/ : two-man action displaces help defender, switch or stay requires communication. Use noncontact cuts and legal blocks only; do not import physical picks as blanket permission. Canadian Lacrosse coaching manual https://bclacrosse.com/Coaching%20Clinics/Coaching%20Clinic%20Manuals/Community%20Development%20Men%27s%20Field%20Lacrosse%20Manual%20-%20EN%20-%20Fillable.pdf describes successive help rotations replacing vacated coverage.
- FIH Moses Kagochi https://academy.fih.hockey/course/tcms1e5 : half-court press, triggers and variation; landing-page outline, not full recording. NFHS/USA Field Hockey https://assets.nfhs.org/umbraco/media/1015440/coaching-field-hockey.pdf identifies channeling, double teams, outlet, transfer, counterattack as teaching topics; outline only. Our trap and outlet designs below are original.
- IHF coaching booklet https://archive.ihf.info/files/Uploads/Documents/7869_090525_Challenge_Trophy_GB.pdf p18: 3v3 attack with feeding player, crossings and pivot, depth, exchange of marking. Adapt crossing and pivot role, no illegal screen. Handball seminar https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcrY5TbPykM describes backcourt/pivot cooperation drills; video not independently viewed.
- Previous football coaching casebook supplies Kiffin pace, Walsh diagnostic scripts, Leach mastery, Parcells complementary football, Belichick opponent plans, Johnson reads, Saban/Snyder staff learning and development, Reid paired looks. Implement those principles through these concrete books too; do not leave them as profile prose.

## Coaching notation and execution contract
Z Zeroback; V Viper; H Halfback; W Wingback; S Slotback; F fourth flanker; L1-L5 line players. Left/right mirrored relative to offense. A 'channel' is left edge, left interior, middle, right interior, right edge; 'depth' is relative to line and current ball, not a new field dimension. Five L initially on line unless a legal alternative alignment is explicitly verified. Other six behind/on permitted line. Default Z receives snap; alternate snapper only if legal and implemented. Receivers of hand transfer must be level or behind the ball at transfer; one kick pass maximum. Read means an observable defender commitment with imperfect player recognition, not knowledge of opponent's future random result. Decoy means run to threaten space with no illegal contact. 'Switch' can be across downs, or backward/level lateral(s) inside a play; no forward throw. Each call lists initial look, actions, branch, fallback, weakness and counter. A look is observable, action is private until executed. Do not grant yards from look name. Eligibility includes available healthy personnel, role skill, installed familiarity, legal state and actual caller; fallback to normal selector if unsupported. Mirror both sides, log actual side.

Three implementation levels: A = existing legal selection and package tags (verify exact enum keys and catalog vocabulary; no new family needed). B = new sequence, observable context, role, or assignment primitive in selector/resolver; use existing physical contests. C = material rule/engine gap; must implement and validate mechanics or leave disabled, never claim a placeholder is executable. Levels below are initial judgments to verify. Proposed schools are distinct conditional decision policies; integrate new school IDs, catalog families, matchup handling, recruiting, staff inheritance, UI and saves only once enough constituent calls really work. Add calls to compatible existing schools first, then activate full new schools. Do not add arbitrary outcome multipliers.

## Offensive book A: Free Motion (CFL + A-11 + AFL + soccer)
Identity: threaten multiple distributors, speed after snap, interchangeable receiving/support roles. Compatible existing Ghost, Mismatch, Stampede, Slick 'n' Slide, Lateral Spread. Prefer Z/V/W/S/F awareness, hands, agility, speed, kicking; OL protection and stamina; keep two-way defense viable. Distinguishes itself from Stampede (pace alone) and Ghost (Viper misdirection alone).
- FM01 Switchback [B]: spread; W and S cross at staggered depths after snap, Z carries toward weak seam; if defender follows W, attack vacated lane with S via legal lateral or single kick pass depending depth; if defenders switch cleanly, Z keeps or normal quick outlet. No crossing at illegal pre-snap speed. Counter exchange coverage; risk collision/timing, pressure on Z. 
- FM02 Twin Trigger [C/B]: split; Z and trained H are legal alternate snap recipients, caller chooses recipient before snap; nonrecipient trails as outlet; choose carry or legal single kick pass based on local coverage. Default Z if snap alternative unsupported. Counter contain both without extra defenders; risk bad snap, weaker kicker. Cannot mark live until actual snap recipient can change; do not fake via featured-position bias.
- FM03 False Lead [A/B]: spread; V threatens one edge, W attacks opposite seam; Z selects counter/sweep or quick kick pass after observable overcommitment. Existing counter bias can be first version, but directional and decoy tracking required for full design. Counter disciplined zone. Risk wasted decoy, long release.
- FM04 Third Runner [B]: split/spread; Z single kick pass to S ahead, W trails S at catch, S laterals level/back to W only if lane opens, otherwise secure and run. Use kick-lateral resolver if genuinely models this chain. Counter cover second runner; risk additional turnover, interception cancels pending bonus.
- FM05 Anchor Release [C/B]: tight; one line player releases only after initial protection responsibility, becomes level/back outlet if under pressure; other four maintain actual protection, receiver carries if clear. Counter rush vacated lane. Risk less protection and low handling. No automatic fifth blocker or illegal pick. Requires actual OL participation and legal transition.
- FM06 Mirror Flood [B]: spread; V/S/F threaten one side at staggered depths, H remains opposite; Z chooses one legal target or run, not multiple kick passes. If defense overloads, mirror next down with previous response as weak evidence. Counter balanced deep/under coverage. Risk pressure and weak-side isolation.
Install progression: FM03 existing counterpart, FM04 real kick-lateral, then FM01/06, then alternate distributor/releasing lineman. Practice timing 3-player groups and switching defenders; measure receiver/distributor diversity conditional on look, actual targeted lane, turnover and pressure.

## Offensive book B: Phase Attack (league + union + Gaelic)
Identity: build a sequence over six-down sets to move defenders, secure support, attack edge and finish according to state. Compatible Ground Control, Slick 'n' Slide, Lateral Spread, Mousetrap, Six Shooter. Prefer durable H/F/OL, W/V agility, Z awareness, reliable kicker. Distinct from pure possession and long lateral chain.
- PH01 Pod Carry [A/B]: tight; H runs interior with legal blocking and two trailing support outlets, no wedge or assisting runner. If interior congested choose existing counter/option; do not invent ruck. Counter inside wall; risk limited yardage.
- PH02 Same-Side Reload [B]: after previous legal PH01 and favorable observable response, attack same channel with fresh eligible runner; if defense compresses use PH03/04. No automatic defensive movement just because last call ran there. Counter rotate fresh defenders; risk repetitive tackles/fatigue.
- PH03 Pullback [B]: split; H threatens interior, actual ball goes backward to Z or S behind H, distributor chooses run or one kick pass. If pressure closes outlet, secure ball and carry. Counter hold middle/edge depth, pressure distributor. Risk extra transfer.
- PH04 Open Edge [B]: after interior or one-side concentration observed, opposite W/V edge run with trailing F; use sweep or lateral; if opposite edge not open, choose normal safe advancement. Counter maintain weak-side defender; risk isolated runner.
- PH05 Short-Side Strike [B]: after attack ends near hash, use shorter edge if numerically favorable; Z/H run or legal quick lateral to V, no assumption engine tracks exact ball lateral coordinate (hash/side proxy if available). Counter keep boundary contain. Risk sideline and insufficient space.
- PH06 Finish Read [A policy integration]: on fourth/fifth/sixth down evaluate actual remaining distance, kick range, clock, score, miss/return risk and legal choices. Use current finish selector; not catalog scoring play. Do not force kick on scheduled down; if new set, reset. Counter Keeper Hunt or rush line when kick plausible. 
Example set: PH01, PH02, PH03, PH04, PH06; branch based on observed response, legal state, fatigue and conversion. A first down resets set; turnover/score/half clears; penalty only advances state if valid play counts; retain logged opponent tendencies across sets with decay. Train 3-phase sequences against shifting defense. Measure within-set transitions, conditional edge choice, conversion and six-channel outcomes.

## Offensive book C: Relay (soccer + Gaelic + AFL)
Identity: a connected support network, reliable third-player release and side switching, not maximum pace. Compatible East Coast, Lateral Spread, Balanced (compatible only; not package origin), Mismatch. Good Z accuracy, receiving and lateral skills across two-way line, stamina and awareness.
- RL01 Wall Return [B]: split; Z sends legal lateral to H level/back, H carries and laterals back to trailing Z if defender commits; Z runs or single kick pass to V if still legal. If return lane closed H keeps. Counter staggered coverage. Risk double handling.
- RL02 Up-Back-Across [B]: kick pass to W, W laterals backward to S, S carries across or laterals level to F; no second kick pass. If W under immediate tackle retain. Counter second-layer containment. Risk deep kick interception.
- RL03 Anchor Switch [B]: tight/split; H receives legal lateral, returns to central Z behind ball, Z advances opposite side or one kick pass to V. Counter weak-side discipline; risk lost ground/clock.
- RL04 Lead and Crumb [B/C]: V times lead to catch kick pass while S is in legal support position for catch-and-lateral or live muff only. Grounded incomplete kick pass remains dead; do not award bells for ordinary incompletion. Counter deep coverage and loose-ball pursuit. Risk interception or actual loose ball.
- RL05 Safe Outlet [A/B]: pressure look, Z quick kick pass or safe lateral to H/S behind; prioritize legal low-exposure choice when bonus pending. Counter cover shallow lane and controlled pressure; risk low advancement.
Distinct from Chain Gang: short controlled combinations with escape/fallback, low transfer count when danger high. Train triangle timing, pressure outlet and one-kick limit. Metrics: support involvement, lateral depth legality, release under pressure, turnovers conditional on transfers, successful side changes.

## Offensive book D: Crossfire (lacrosse + handball + soccer)
Identity: move defenders out of preferred assignments without illegal picks; rotate focal attacker and exploit defender handoffs. Compatible Ghost, Mousetrap, Mismatch, East Coast. Agile V/S/W, aware Z, reliable H pivot and legal blocking.
- CF01 Slip Cross [B]: two noncontact intersecting routes at different times/depths; V clears inside while S attacks behind defender's attention; legal run/kick-pass/lateral based on relative depth. Counter switch/stay call. Risk timing and route collision.
- CF02 Pivot Turn [B]: H shows central carry, trails as a safe outlet after Z attacks edge; if defender steps toward Z use legal backward transfer to H; otherwise Z keeps. Counter help defender remains inside. Risk slow play and pressure.
- CF03 Double Clear [B]: two decoys threaten opposite directions; actual V runs through newly opened seam, receive one kick pass or carry on legal lateral. Counter zone with layered cover. Risk multiple noncarriers and weak protection.
- CF04 Handoff Punish [B]: call only when prior observations show switch mismatch; choose receiving threat against weaker eligible defender using actual contest; if no evidence, revert. Counter reassign and help. Risk noisy scouting.
- CF05 Same-Look Reverse [A/B]: pair CF01 or CF02 look with opposite counter on subsequent eligible down; log look ID separately from action. No automatic counter bonus.
Train 2v2/3v3 crossings and communication. Metrics: actual assignment swaps, uncovered lanes, mismatch selection, legality, protection tradeoff. No body picks unless explicitly legal under current rules; illegal screen, wedge and blindside enforcement preserved.

## Offensive book E: Territory and Transition (AFL + CFL + soccer + rugby)
This is a package family first, not automatically a new school: already overlaps Ironclad, Boot Raid, Quick Strike and Six Shooter.
- TT01 Lead Outlet [A/B]: on a difficult start or after live turnover, quick kick pass to receiver leading into available space or legal safe run if rushed. Counter press outlet. Risk interception and bonus cost.
- TT02 Transition Burst [B]: immediately after legally recorded fast break or favorable turnover, choose prepared rapid attack while opponent changes legally; do not create new fast-break rule. Counter recovery coverage; risk own players tired or missing anchors.
- TT03 Territory Trap [A policy]: when score/clock/down permits punt or long kick, choose legal existing action with actual return/pindown risk and coverage assignment; no arbitrary pinned start. Counter Keeper return/outlet; risk lost possession.
- TT04 Protect Bonus [A/B policy]: when bonus pending, prefer lower turnover exposure given scoring need and clock; preserve actual cancel-on-fumble/interception rules. Counter disciplined pressure. Risk too conservative when trailing.
- TT05 Delta Entry [A policy]: on power-play/penalty-kill restart assess actual start, scoring range and time; coordinate six-down plan rather than blanket bonus. Counter territory or kick pressure. Risk misjudging future restart. Use current expected-points disclosure; do not claim validated counterfactual values.
Train transition return, legal substitution timing and two-way work rate. Measure real return and restart distributions, pindowns, bells, bonus conversion and clock.

## Defensive faculty: complementary packages that MUST ship with attacks
Existing packages and school compatibility must be checked; reuse an equivalent rather than duplicate. Each package allocates existing defenders to distinct assignments; if one helps, an actual other assignment is vacated. No extra rusher, duplicate defender, magical coverage or automatic takeaways.
- D01 Exchange (Lockdown/Chaos/Shadow): against FM01, CF01/03, two defenders switch responsibilities only when cross occurs and communication/awareness allows. Stay when threat not crossed. Hold deep support. Cost vulnerability to fake crosses and quick central run. Log actual swaps.
- D02 Stagger (Drift/Lockdown): against FM04, RL02, keep shallow ball pressure, middle support-lane defender and deepest receiver coverage at distinct depths. Concede some run space. No same player in multiple layers. Log second-runner coverage.
- D03 Edge Anchor (Swarm/Fortress): against PH02/04 and RL03, retain weak-side contain while central unit tackles. If edge attack expected, shift one actual defender, not whole unit. Cost weaker inside wall. Log strong/weak-side assignments.
- D04 Slide and Replace (Shadow/Rotation): when primary defender beaten, eligible help defender takes ball path, next defender covers vacated threat, beaten defender recovers if physically able. Do not double count tackle or grant free interception. Cost open far channel, fatigue. Use on CF02/04 and FM06.
- D05 Channel Press (Blitz Pack/Keeper Hunt): pressure distributor toward chosen boundary, nearest cover denies safe outlet, deepest keeps explosive coverage. Trigger on isolated recipient or observable poor protection; never read hidden action. Concede far-side switch and interior run. Real rush count and kick pressure stats. Source soccer/hockey press; not unlimited pursuit.
- D06 Transition Net (Rotation/Drift, coordinate STC): on live turnover return nearest available players legally tackle/cover likely return lanes; others recover depth, preserve legal substitutions. If ball dead only prepare next snap. No automatic recovery. Cost fewer aggressive return blockers after change; log live/dead distinction.
- D07 Cross Rush (Blitz Pack/Chaos): CFL-inspired two assigned rushers exchange lanes at snap, others retain commitments; if blocker passes off cleanly no advantage. Cost delayed arrival and open draw lane. Require actual lane/assignment resolver before enabling; pressure credited only to participant.
- D08 Set Breaker (Fortress/Swarm/Chaos): use observed early-down sequences to vary commitment on later downs, keep uncertainty; force offense to abandon script. Costs preparation and exposure if inference wrong. No direct knowledge of planned next call. Counter PH and CF05.
Defensive adoption uses existing installation and roster tags; test against unrelated offense too. Include ST returns/coverage, not new ST school by default.
