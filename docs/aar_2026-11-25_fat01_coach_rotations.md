# FAT-01 — Coach-dependent shifts and snap continuity

CCH-08 precedes this change on the same college continuation branch. Source inspection found that _credit_snap_counts charged energy and then rotated shifts before resolving the very same play. The participant credited with the snap could therefore differ from the participant who actually contested it. Individual position swaps also evaluated multiple replacements against an unchanged unavailable list, so a single fresh backup could authorize more than one resting starter.

Move rotation to the beginning of the following play, after the prior snap and injury exposure resolve. Preserve the existing live-transition marker and caught-changing mechanism. Recalculate the eligible XI after every accepted swap and verify a real qualified replacement without losing a field slot; roll back an invalid swap. Blend head coach rotation attribute (60%) with unit coordinator (40%; midfield averages OC/DC). Center at 60, cap at +/-1, change out thresholds by at most six energy points and quality tolerance by at most four percentage points. No unconditional replacement or extra recovery bonus; close-game starter ride and work rate remain active. Expose per-unit preference, individual shifts and credited snap counts in game-day data; include players with participation but no box-score event.

These changes affect the shared full engine, including other modes that use its rotation system; no separate pro/international rules or roster changes. Runtime effects on fatigue, injuries, score and depth remain unmeasured pending integrated validation. Corrected the CCH-08 focused test fixture's required CoachCard attributes in this commit.
