# Offensive packages from other codes

Added five offensive packages to the regular call path and the Cookbook. Kick and Follow uses the short kick contest, then reads the matched defender and the trailing runner to choose a keep or one lateral. Boundary Switch and Carry, Carry, Release track two matching setup calls within a possession. Observed pressure can trigger a kick or sweep to the opposite side. The engine represents the switch as a kick from the next snap. Protect the Bonus uses inside carries and short kick passes while the offense still has an extra drive pending. Corner Chase puts a real coverage runner on a directional punt in eligible sixth down situations. It can compete with a lower value scoring kick when the offense is tied or ahead by at most five points.

The diagrams show the variants when a concept has more than one action. The existing defense, weather, penalties and ball handling still affect results. Existing schools and package eligibility remain the entry point for team assignments.

## Verification

All 343 tests in the full suite passed. Chromium verified the five previews, all four variant controls, animation, and saving and reopening a book. Engine tests exercise the keep and lateral branches, setup and payoff, bonus eligibility, and the directional punt path.

In a small Alabama and Duke smoke sample with full package familiarity, 16 games per package produced 302 Kick and Follow keeps and 60 support laterals, 143 switches, 201 releases, and two directional punts. Neither directional punt scored a pindown.

The bonus comparison replayed 32 seeds with and without Protect the Bonus. The control reached 70 extra drives from 78 pending opportunities and scored 8.49 points per opportunity across both drives. The package reached 61 extra drives from 72 opportunities and scored 8.71 points per opportunity. Because the calls changed the states that led to interceptions, those groups had different opportunities. The sample shows a cost in bonus retention and does not establish a general advantage.
