# AAR — CCH-09 coaching career and program leadership implementation

Design discussion: [CCH-09 report](PRD_CCH09_COACHING_CAREERS_AND_PROGRAM_LEADERSHIP.md). Implemented on the continuation of #378; main at the beginning was 4968495. Prior reduced calibration and one national seed passed; second national seed was still running at last check. No claim that CCH-09 has passed yet.

## Implementation record

- Career state persisted separately from stable tactical profile: tier, identity-seeded interest rate, fractional attribute gains, role-year counts, management profile, ambition, reputation, completed season evidence. Historical role years inferred only from completed recorded stints, not invented from aggregate wins. First HC appointment unlocks a profile; return to coordinator deactivates it. Existing HCs lazily receive a profile. Actual caller and authority feed OC/DC/STC resolvers and team installation. Former HCs require calls when accepting coordinator work; a simultaneous new HC appointment respects that condition. No interpersonal conflict system.
- Readiness now advances with preparation and relevant experience; never forces ambition. Portal considers reputation along with role ability, readiness and existing hiring preferences; salary uses target role, bounded reputation premium and veteran coordinator premium. Accomplishment and roster-relative results update reputation with .22 annual weighting. No reputation win bonus. Pregame expectation is a provisional logistic top-22 roster overall comparison, home offset .12, spread scale 12. It is not calibrated probability or DTW. Legacy games missing pregame expectation use accomplishments only.
- Stable 75/20/5 rate tiers, inverse annual cap, role-specific attribute focus and fractional gain carry. No intrinsic growth penalty from losing, age slows learning; older coaches may lose instincts/rotations. Positive overachievement can modestly accelerate growth. The constants are provisional. Existing six attributes, classifications and tactical identity remain.
- Management development/recruiting emphasis enters existing program-level calculations, bounded; former HCs provide up to .75 extra permanent unit development points with experience, plus up to .75 from coordinator teaching. No additional game-day experience bonus. Existing development trait and rating caps remain authoritative; do not infer measured performance yet. STC coaching authority is wired; permanent unit teaching currently maps position groups to OC/DC, not a third stacked STC boost.
- Focused tests and existing integrated workflow extended; dynasty transition reports include career evidence. Need 20–30-year career/market and actual program outcome validation, including staffing and positional development, market prestige and promotion distribution, historical save parity, repeatability, role movement and rating inflation.

## Known limits

The pregame roster-only expectation is not opponent-adjusted beyond the two rosters and home offset, does not measure coordinator causal contribution and needs calibration. Management profile retains staff/continuity/game-management descriptors; staffing affinity, succession policy and explicit management-specific clock/rotation decisions are not separately wired here beyond the existing HC effects and authority. Role experience is known only from recorded stints; generated histories without role detail are not fabricated. Manual season hiring uses authority check, marketplace without team staff context uses appointment/experience but cannot negotiate an incumbent's play-calling preference. No new conflict, pro/international, or cross-division competition model. Existing college portal supports smaller-program jobs within its current league only. Long-term career distributions, deployment and UI presentation of career data remain outstanding.

## Follow-up integration

Desired program prestige now derives from market reputation and independently stored ambition rather than unbounded cumulative trophies. Existing defensive gameplan preparation follows the actual DC caller; existing clock management blends the active HC management profile at 15%. API exposes readiness, reputation, known role years, active management and actual play callers without exposing the hidden career growth rate. Game tactical summaries distinguish caller from employed coordinator. Stats-site career pages remain follow-up. This adds no new clock system or general game outcome bonus.
