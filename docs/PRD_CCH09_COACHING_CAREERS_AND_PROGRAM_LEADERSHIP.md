# CCH-09 — Coaching careers, development, authority and program leadership

## Design discussion and intent (November 25, 2026)

The coach is a persistent person, not a role. Becoming a head coach for the first time unlocks a separate management profile. Returning to coordination makes that profile dormant; later head appointments reactivate it. No demotion resentment, culture clashes, morale disputes or personality conflict simulation. Preserve the original specialty, acquired tactical knowledge and accumulated experience.

Head coaches can be play callers, system builders, developers, program administrators, game managers, delegators and recruiters. These are overlapping tendencies, not exclusive classes. Head coaching decisions affect the program: delegation, staffing, scheme continuity, player development, recruiting priorities, rotations, clock and risk management. A play-calling HC and employed coordinator are different jobs; record who actually makes the call. Former HCs can negotiate for play-calling authority and are more valuable as assistants. Their broader experience can enhance permanent position-specific player development without directly adding a win bonus.

- [Tennis coaching growth](https://github.com/quarterback/tennis-team-manager/blob/6e04fabc17f95cc0d1ad450aabeedf8a48e3c364/docs/AAR-coach-development-growth.md): permanent growth multiplier .70–1.30 anchored to actual development ratings (35/50/65), not match dice. Borrow durable rates, diminishing annual growth, asymmetry and cumulative program outcomes, not literal baseball constants or a replacement player model.

## Distinct quantities

Ability = existing six attributes plus tactical knowledge. Aptitude = role-specific ability calculation, not a new permanent grade. Ambition = desire for a job; readiness = experience for its responsibilities. Reputation = market evidence, not underlying competence. A talented coach at a poor program must not be intrinsically punished for its record. An average assistant with sustained success at a prominent program should become attractive to employers. Accomplishment (wins, titles, postseason, recognition) and performance relative to pregame expectations both matter. The latter is a proxy, not proof of individual causation. Reputation should influence offers, pay and opportunities, not magically improve game-day performance.

## Career trajectories

Traditional successful major-program assistant → head coach remains common. Smaller-program head coach → progressively stronger jobs, late-career breakthrough, excellent lifelong coordinator, former HC returning to specialist work, celebrated hire underperforming, and unrecognized coach transforming a weak program must all be possible through ordinary mechanisms, not scripted events. The Cignetti and DeBoer examples illustrate paths through smaller programs and subsequent moves; no literal reproduction or forced frequency. No compulsory HC ambition threshold. Distinguish readiness from demand and actual aptitude.

A stable coaching interest rate changes existing attributes over decades, shaped by role experience. Head coaching develops leadership, recruiting, game management and broader capabilities; coordinator work develops specialist preparation and teaching. Performance may modestly accelerate learning but poor team results should not cause ability regression. Growth should be bounded, with aging and meaningful differences in trajectories. Rates and tiers need calibration at 20–30 years, not four college seasons.

## Program transformation

Effective leadership → improved teaching, recruiting and personnel decisions → stronger returning players → better competitive outcomes → reputation and prestige → better staff and recruiting opportunities. A development-centered program can improve gradually, a recruitment-centered program can attract talent faster. Neither guarantees winning; roster gaps and staff skill matter. Program improvements should survive departures through actual roster strength, installation and continuity, not an invisible coach-win multiplier. Experienced coordinators should strengthen their unit's permanent development. No duplicate boost applied both as temporary game-day aura and career experience.
