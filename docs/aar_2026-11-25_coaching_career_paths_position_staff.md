# AAR: career paths and position staff

November 25, 2026. Implements the approved plan in docs/plan_coaching_career_paths_position_staff.md.

## Changes

- Durable career intent stored in existing CoachCard.career_development. Special teams uses 65% specialist / 25% coordinator-track / 10% head-coach-track draws; OC/DC uses 75/20/5 specialist/secondary/generalist. Qualifications require experience and aptitude; eligibility does not force a hire. New-discipline calling authority begins at .65 and grows with completed seasons. The old recurring 30% eligibility lottery is removed.
- Six teaching assignments are covered by existing coordinators or dedicated position coaches. Prestige bands fund two, four or six dedicated slots. Each position coach has a unique card, contracts, pay, career records, and a teaching or advancement preference. Existing contracts are retained after prestige drops. The incremental salary cap is $85,000 + $1,800 times prestige per new/renewed dedicated contract; existing senior payroll and legacy budget logic are unchanged.
- Qualified employed assistants can seek coordinator promotions without resigning first. Vacancies try compatible available candidates before generated replacements. Actual departing players can enter a delayed position-coach pool with stable player-linked IDs. They are not guaranteed employment.
- Specialist technical instruction redistributes an existing positive development ceiling allowance; the sum of the ceilings is conserved and extra concentration is capped at .75 points per attribute. No new player attribute or live snap multiplier. Staff expertise changes basic scouting error and CPU targeting by at most 3%; teaching recommendations are advisory. Staff relationship retention requires recorded tenure and is capped at .025. Installation uses existing familiarity with at most .02 additional offseason base-book learning; position coaches receive a small bounded development effect from their actual unit mentor.
- Both interactive and standalone recruiting paths use the staff. Staff cards, coverage, payroll and career history are exposed through existing endpoints; stats-site staff and coach pages include named assignments. Existing coach and dynasty serialization stores the new data without a separate database schema.
- Tests in existing coach-career suite cover stable career intent, earned crossover and authority recovery, position promotion and unique employment, staffing size/payroll, save/load, conserved development, scouting/targeting, and alumni delay/deduplication. Longitudinal benchmark now records actual role trajectories, STC retention, crossovers, promotions, salary distributions, generated hires, alumni and existing player development/overall distributions.

## Boundaries and calibration

All percentages and caps are proposed parameters, not measured outcomes. The 5–10% lifetime OC/DC crossover rate is an outcome to assess from 20–30-year runs, not guaranteed by the eligibility draws. The per-slot position salary allowance does not repair the existing senior-staff budget mismatch. No dedicated position coach play-call or depth-chart override. Recruiting remains a small targeting modifier; it is not a new recruit signing preference. The existing offseason and career benchmarks must be run with multiple seeds against baseline to assess long-run mobility, pay, development and talent distributions. No local full-suite run claimed: this runtime cannot clone the repository. The interactive and engine paths share the new position staff logic; the concurrent Step 7 PR #383 may need reconciliation because it also touches dynasty, recruiting and reporting.
